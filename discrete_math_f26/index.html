Discrete math is the core building block of computer science. Algorithms, networks, cryptography, databases, AI, and even social networks all have discrete math hiding underneath. In this class we'll learn to turn our intuition about why things work into rigorous logical statements. Along the way we'll learn how to count the number of pizza orders you can make with four toppings, and, perhaps most importantly, how to set the right lineup to win your fantasy football league.
