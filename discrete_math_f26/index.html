Description:

Discrete Math is the core building block of computer science. Algorithms, networks, cryptography, databases, AI, and even social networks all have discrete math hiding underneath. In this class, we’ll learn to formalize our intuition about why things work into rigorous logical statements. Along the way, we’ll learn how to count the number of different pizza orders you can make with just four toppings, and perhaps most importantly, how to set the right lineup to win your fantasy football league.
